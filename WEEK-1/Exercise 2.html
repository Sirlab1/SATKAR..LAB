Playing badminton

Why I Love It

badminton is my favourite hobby because I am very good at it and enjoy competing with other players. I love improving my skills, especially smash, and receiving, and I always try to perform my best.

How to Start

To start playing badminton, you need a racket, a suttle cork, and a suitable place for practice. You should learn the basic rules and practise technique regularly to become a better player.
